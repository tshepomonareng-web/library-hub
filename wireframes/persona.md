# Persona: Thandi Mokoena, 34, screen reader user

**Background:** Blind since age 12. Works as a call-centre trainer in Johannesburg and uses the public library for study and community events.

**Technical configuration**
- Windows 11 laptop with NVDA 2025 and Firefox; iPhone with VoiceOver and Safari
- Braille display (Focus 40) for proofreading forms
- Input: keyboard only on the laptop, touch gestures on the phone; no mouse

**Navigation habits:** jumps by landmarks (D), headings (H) and form fields (F); uses the elements list to scan links.

**Pain points**
- Unlabelled inputs and "click here" links
- Errors shown only in red text and never announced
- Modals that lose focus or trap it
- Filters that change results silently
- CAPTCHAs and time-limited forms

**Goals**
1. Find a course and register without sighted help
2. Reserve a study room and receive an announced confirmation
3. Filter the digital archive and know how many results remain
